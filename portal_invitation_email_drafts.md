# JUMP 2026 private-portal invitation drafts

These are the three package-specific email structures for the initial owner test. Each recipient receives only their own personal portal link, no PDF attachment. Their portal opens on a personalised engagement brief and requires a digital acknowledgement before the assessment, payment guidance and eligible scheduling steps become available.

## Foundation test email

**Subject:** JUMP 2026 — Your private portal and Foundation engagement brief

Dear **[First Name]**,

I trust this meets you well and in good health.

Thank you again for registering for JUMP 2026. I am pleased to share your private participant portal for **[Business Name]** and your selected **Foundation** pathway.

**Your private portal:** [Personal portal link]

When you open the link, the first thing you will see is your personalised engagement brief. It explains the advisory model, your Foundation access, the programme calendar, recordings and flexibility arrangements, the 40/30/30 payment structure, and the steps ahead. Kindly take the time to read it through and select **“I have read and consent to the terms.”**

That acknowledgement is the digital confirmation that you have received, understood and are ready to proceed with the engagement. You will receive a dated email confirmation immediately after you acknowledge it. The portal will then open your Current State Assessment, which is the first step in giving me the colour and detail I need to shape the advisory work around your business.

The programme is currently scheduled to open on **Friday, 4 September 2026**. Every call will be recorded. Where an unforeseen change in my schedule is necessary, I will communicate it at least 72 hours ahead and provide a rescheduled date. I also remain flexible in ensuring that participants can catch up; where there is significant absence, we may organise an additional make-up class.

Your Foundation fee is **₦575,000**: a 40% commitment payment of **₦230,000** before classes begin, 30% by the end of September, and the final 30% by mid-October. A 10% full-upfront discount brings the total to **₦517,500**. Direct-transfer details will be confirmed within the portal, and a Paystack route for international payments will be available before the end of the week.

The platform has been designed specifically for this programme and will continue to improve as we work. If you encounter any technical difficulty, kindly take a screenshot and email me directly. I read every participant email and will have the technical team look into it promptly.

I look forward to the work ahead.

Warm regards,

Emmanuel Tarfa  
Facilitator, JUMP 2026 — Strategy & Innovation Genius Track
