## Package-specific paragraph options

### Foundation

The Foundation package gives you the complete five-class programme, live Q&A and post-class question time, slides and replays, curated resources, and *The Last Testament of Business* textbook. It is the right starting point for a founder who wants a rigorous structure for better strategic choices.

### Engine Room

The Engine Room package includes everything in Foundation, together with two additional sessions grouped by the way your business makes money — Maker, Trader, or Expert. It brings more direct work on competitive positioning, peer diagnosis, unit economics, and the practical application of the frameworks to your business model.

### Boardroom

The Boardroom package includes all Foundation and Engine Room access, plus three private 90-minute strategy sessions with me and written action points after each session. It is deliberately limited to eight businesses so that the individual work remains substantive. The higher-tier access is cumulative: it is not a separate payment for the Foundation experience.
