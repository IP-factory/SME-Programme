# JUMP Google OAuth Production Publication

**Verified:** 20 August 2026

The Google Cloud project **JUMP 2026 Communications** now shows its OAuth publishing status as **In production** on the Google Auth Platform Audience page. The presence of the **Back to testing** control confirms that the owner-approved publication action was completed.

The page also displays a Google notice that the app requires verification. This does not reverse the production publication. It is a separate Google verification consideration that may be relevant if the application is distributed beyond the current authorised use case or requests sensitive scopes. The JUMP platform’s previously restored Google Calendar connection should remain under review through the existing calendar readiness checks.

**Source:** https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/audience?project=jump-2026-communications
