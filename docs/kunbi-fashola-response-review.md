## Why This Is the Right Response

The reply directly corrects the central ambiguity without making Kunbi feel that she has misunderstood the programme. It validates the likely intention behind her question—a pre-programme opportunity to understand the experience—while clearly positioning Foundation as a shared group advisory engagement. It also protects the distinction that has commercial and delivery implications: private strategy sessions are a Boardroom benefit, not a Foundation entitlement.

## Recommended Website Improvement

Kunbi’s question signals that the portal should differentiate the *delivery model* of each pathway, not only its content. The public programme overview and the authenticated Programme tab should make the shared-versus-private distinction unmistakable before a participant starts the assessment.

| Portal location | Recommended addition | Participant benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **Programme tab — Foundation** | **“A shared group advisory experience: five live applied strategy classes, group questions, recordings and practical resources.”** | Prevents an expectation of a private pre-programme call while retaining the premium, applied character of the offer. |
| **Programme tab — Engine Room** | **“Foundation plus deeper commercial-model and operating work in focused advisory sessions.”** | Explains the added depth without implying that it is the same as Boardroom’s private strategy access. |
| **Programme tab — Boardroom** | **“The cumulative engagement: Foundation and Engine Room access, plus three private 90-minute strategy sessions and written action points.”** | Makes the private-session entitlement explicit and protects the value of the highest pathway. |
| **Current State Assessment** | **“This is an opening diagnostic, not the whole conversation. We will ask further questions ahead of relevant classes as the work develops.”** | Reassures every participant that the assessment is a beginning, not an exhaustive assignment. |
| **Sessions tab — Foundation** | A **Foundation Welcome & Information Session** status card, marked **“Proposed for Friday 28 August — time to be confirmed.”** | Gives Foundation participants a clear pre-programme orientation point without promising an individual meeting. |
| **Sessions tab — Engine Room / Boardroom** | Package-specific wording for their additional sessions; only Boardroom describes private 90-minute strategy sessions. | Keeps booking and session expectations aligned with the selected pathway. |

I recommend publishing the pathway descriptions in the Programme tab immediately. The Foundation welcome-session card should become live only after Emmanuel confirms the time and delivery format. The booking language should remain package-specific, and participant self-booking should not be released until the Google Calendar integration has been re-authorised and tested.
