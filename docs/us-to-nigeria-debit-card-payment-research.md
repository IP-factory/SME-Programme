## Critical operational points

- Nigerian recipients receive **NGN**, not USD, under the cited Nigeria corridors; transfers to domiciliary USD accounts are not available through Sendwave and Taptap Send states it follows the Central Bank restriction.
- “Instant” is never guaranteed: first transfers and higher-value payments may trigger identity, fraud, source-of-funds, or card-issuer checks.
- Do not share banking details in ordinary email. Give the payer the beneficiary name exactly as held by the Nigerian bank and ask them to confirm the recipient amount shown at checkout before paying.
- For business or programme payments, use the provider's business product or formal payment route where available, and preserve the provider receipt and the payer's invoice/reference for reconciliation.
